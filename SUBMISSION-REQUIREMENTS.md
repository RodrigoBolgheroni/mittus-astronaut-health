# Mittus Submission Requirements — NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

## Deadlines and eligibility

- Mittus is registered for the [Campinas event](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/local-events/campinas/). The portal submission window opens on **Saturday, November 14, 2026 at 9:00 a.m.** and closes on **Sunday, November 15, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Brasília time**.
- To be eligible for awards, confirm that all six Mittus team members are registered for the same event, are not on the waitlist, and appear under the team's **Members** tab. For a local event, confirm judging and awards times with the **Local Lead**; they may follow a separate schedule.

## What to prepare

1. **Project page in English.** Complete all required fields under the **Project** tab. Linked materials must also be in English, except for the audio exception described below.
2. **Required demonstration.** Submit either a video of **up to 30 seconds** or a presentation of **up to 7 slides**, including the title and introduction. The video may have Portuguese audio with **English subtitles**. The presentation must be in English. Exceeding the selected format's limit makes the project ineligible for global judging.
3. **Public links.** Host the demo and project files on external services and test the links in a private browser window. Access must work without login, registration, or permission requests. The project page accepts JPG, PNG, and GIF images up to 10 MB. Videos and documents must be linked.
4. **Originality and imagery.** Confirm that the work is original in the form. The demo and final project must not include images, voice recordings, videos, or other depictions of people under 18.

## Project tab fields

Use the **What to prepare** column as a guide for Mittus. Check required fields and text limits in the form itself.

| Portal field | What to prepare |
| --- | --- |
| Project Name | Project name. |
| High-Level Project Summary | Summary of the problem, what the software does, and its benefit to astronauts. |
| Link to Project Demo | Public link to the video or slides. |
| Link to Final Project | Public link to the prototype, repository with access instructions, or another place where judges can review the submission. |
| Detailed Project Description | Description of the user flow, what has been implemented, and what the prototype demonstrates. |
| Use of Super Intelligence (SI) | State whether AI was used and describe where and how the tools were used. |
| NASA Data | Identify the NASA data or resources used and explain how they contributed. At least one NASA data source or resource is required for global awards. |
| Space Agency Partner Data and Other References | Cite external data, tools, and materials used, including code, text, and images, even when free or open source. Use third-party materials with the appropriate permissions and identify applicable licenses in the submission documentation. |

### Use of AI

The guide allows AI as a supporting tool, while the team remains responsible for the solution's design. Label AI-generated images and videos with a **visible watermark**. Disclose AI-generated audio, code, or data **in the descriptive text and metadata**. Do not include NASA marks or visual elements in AI-generated content. [Project Submission Guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/)

## What to show in the demo

Introduce Mittus and the challenge. Show the final software or prototype in use and explain how NASA or partner-agency resources contributed, if used. If you choose slides, include the team members' names.

One possible Mittus flow: show an astronaut logging an indicator, reviewing its history, and finding the software-guided next action. Choose a flow the prototype actually supports.

Judging assigns a score from **1 to 5** for each criterion. Impact considers the solution's benefit; creativity, its originality; validity, its scientific basis, feasibility, and usability; relevance, its response to the challenge and integration of NASA data; and presentation, the clarity of the demo and complete submission. [Judging & Awards Guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/judging-awards-guide/)

## How to submit

1. In the portal, open **My Team → Project → Edit**. Use **Save Progress** to save drafts.
2. Complete the required fields, open the links in a private browser window, and check the duration or slide count, language, and subtitles.
3. Select **Terms and Conditions** and **Confirm All Work is Original**. Click **Submit for Judging** before the deadline.
4. Check the confirmation banner. If you make changes, resubmit before the deadline. Judges evaluate the latest submitted version.
